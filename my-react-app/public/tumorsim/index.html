How it works

  1. Every dot is a cell. Each one eats, ages and sometimes divides in two.
  2. When a cell divides, the copy can pick up a random mutation.
  3. Enough mutations turn a cell into a tumour cell that ignores crowding and keeps dividing.
  4. Immune cells and treatments fight back, but cells that resist them survive and take over.
  5. Cells that learn to move can travel and start new tumours in other organs (see the body map).
